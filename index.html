    Help & Guide

    Quick answers and usage tips

    How do I add a new action item? +

    Click Add New Item in the sidebar, or press N. Fill in Meeting Date, Action Item title, Owner, and other details. Click Save.

    How do I edit an existing item? +

    Go to All Action Items, find the row, and click the Edit button. A modal opens with all fields pre-filled. Make your changes and click Save Changes.

    How does the Calendar View work? +

    The Calendar plots items by their due date. Navigate months with Prev / Next. Dots are colour-coded: yellow = In Progress, green = Completed, grey = Not Started.

    Where is my data stored? +

    All data lives remotely on your designated Excel/Google Sheet via the SheetDB API! Changes are saved automatically as you add, edit, or delete items.

    How do I access meeting documents? +

    Go to the Meeting Documents tab. Paste your OneDrive share link in the settings area. Once saved, you can access all your MOMs and documents directly from the dashboard.
